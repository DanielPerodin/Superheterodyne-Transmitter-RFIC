The circuit works well when the message signal has an amplitude of 500 mV and DC Volatge of 1 V. 
Keep IF = 2 MHz, and feel free to change RF (the local oscillator source) to any frequency between 1.465 MHz and 0.295 MHz to set the transmission to 
any channel within the AM broadcasting range.
